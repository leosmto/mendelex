Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Mendelex is designed as a simple chemistry learning and periodic table reference app. We respect your privacy and aim to keep the app clean, safe and transparent.

Information We Collect

Mendelex does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos or payment information.

Notifications

If you enable optional scientific notifications, they are used to show periodic chemistry facts. Notification permission is handled by the operating system and can be disabled at any time from device settings.

Analytics and Diagnostics

Future versions may use basic, privacy-friendly analytics or crash diagnostics to improve app stability and performance. Any such tools will be used only to improve the product and not to sell personal data.

Third-Party Services

If the app later includes third-party services such as analytics, advertising or cloud features, this policy will be updated before or at the time those services are added.

Children’s Privacy

Mendelex is educational in nature. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not share personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: support@mendelex.app
